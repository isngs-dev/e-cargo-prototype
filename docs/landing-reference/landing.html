06FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Contact Cargo Operations

Demonstration contact — not a monitored address.

Email: cargo.operations@nrc.demo
Who can register?

Companies that provide a CAC number, a CAC certificate and a company profile. A tax certificate may also be requested. During registration you choose your district — Lagos or Ibadan.

When do I pay?

Only after the Director of Operations approves your booking — never when you submit it. You then pay by payment link, or after an NRC-approved credit period.

What are Priority and Non-Priority customers?

They are statuses NRC assigns to a company, not separate logins. The status determines discount, credit and payment options — see the comparison table.

What if my cargo fails the physical check?

The booking is cancelled and its slot is released. If you have already paid, the Director triggers a refund, you submit a refund request, and Super Admin processes it.

What is an AWB?

The Air Waybill issued for your cargo, with a unique number and a QR code you can verify. If details change, a new version is issued and the earlier one is marked superseded.

Is this the live system?

No. This is a prototype. Payments, emails and refunds are simulated, and all data is fictional and stored only in your browser.
